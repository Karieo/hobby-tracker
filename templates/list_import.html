Import a list

Paste it from wherever it came from. One unit per line — counts, points and section headings are all fine, and none of it is saved until you have looked at every line.

Points in the paste are ignored — this app prices a list from the Munitorum manual it imported, so a number copied out of another app can't quietly outrank the official one. Everything else about the shape is forgiving; the names are not. A name either matches a datasheet or comes back for you to decide, and nothing is guessed.
